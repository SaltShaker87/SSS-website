POET (2019)

Partial Oral versus Intravenous Antibiotic Treatment of Endocarditis

Oral step-down was noninferior: primary outcome 9.0% oral vs 12.1% IV, with hospital stay 3 days vs 19 days.

OVIVA (2019)

Oral versus Intravenous Antibiotics for Bone and Joint Infection

Noninferior at 1 year: 13.2% oral vs 14.6% IV treatment failure (risk difference −1.4 pp).
