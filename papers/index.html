Landmark ID papers, visualized

Practice-changing infectious diseases trials, each with a short animated video and recreated charts. Educational summaries, not medical advice.

POET.paper
Bacteremia & duration

POET (2019)

Partial Oral versus Intravenous Antibiotic Treatment of Endocarditis

Oral step-down was noninferior: primary outcome 9.0% oral vs 12.1% IV, with hospital stay 3 days vs 19 days.
OVIVA.paper
Bone & joint

OVIVA (2019)

Oral versus Intravenous Antibiotics for Bone and Joint Infection

Noninferior at 1 year: 13.2% oral vs 14.6% IV treatment failure (risk difference −1.4 pp).
Yahav 7 vs 14.paper
Bacteremia & duration

Yahav 7 vs 14 (2019)

Seven Versus 14 Days of Antibiotic Therapy for Uncomplicated Gram-negative Bacteremia: A Noninferiority Randomized Controlled Trial

7 days was noninferior to 14: primary outcome 45.8% vs 48.3% (risk difference −2.6%).
MODIFY I/II.paper
C. diff

MODIFY I/II (2017)

Bezlotoxumab for Prevention of Recurrent Clostridium difficile Infection

12-week recurrence 17% with bezlotoxumab vs 28% with placebo in MODIFY I (and 16% vs 26% in MODIFY II).
ACTT-1.paper
COVID-19

ACTT-1 (2020)

Remdesivir for the Treatment of Covid-19 — Final Report

Faster recovery: median 10 days with remdesivir vs 15 days with placebo (rate ratio for recovery 1.29).
C4591001.paper
COVID-19

C4591001 (2020)

Safety and Efficacy of the BNT162b2 mRNA Covid-19 Vaccine

95% vaccine efficacy: 8 Covid-19 cases with BNT162b2 vs 162 with placebo.
Nix-TB.paper
TB

Nix-TB (2020)

Treatment of Highly Drug-Resistant Pulmonary Tuberculosis

90% favourable outcome with 26 weeks of all-oral BPaL in XDR or complicated MDR tuberculosis.
RECOVERY.paper
COVID-19

RECOVERY (2021)

Dexamethasone in Hospitalized Patients with Covid-19

28-day mortality 22.9% with dexamethasone vs 25.7% with usual care (rate ratio 0.83); biggest benefit on ventilation.
EPIC-HR.paper
COVID-19

EPIC-HR (2022)

Oral Nirmatrelvir for High-Risk, Nonhospitalized Adults with Covid-19

Hospitalization or death by day 28: 0.72% with nirmatrelvir-ritonavir vs 6.45% with placebo (88.9% relative risk reduction).
MOVe-OUT.paper
COVID-19

MOVe-OUT (2022)

Molnupiravir for Oral Treatment of Covid-19 in Nonhospitalized Patients

Hospitalization or death by day 29: 6.8% with molnupiravir vs 9.7% with placebo (difference −3.0 percentage points).
AMBITION-cm.paper
Fungal

AMBITION-cm (2022)

Single-Dose Liposomal Amphotericin B Treatment for Cryptococcal Meningitis

Noninferior: 10-week mortality 24.8% with single-dose AmBisome vs 28.7% with WHO standard care, with much less anemia.
HPTN 084.paper
HIV

HPTN 084 (2022)

Cabotegravir for the prevention of HIV-1 in women: results from HPTN 084, a phase 3, randomised clinical trial

Superior: HIV incidence 0.2 per 100 person-years with injectable cabotegravir vs 1.85 with daily TDF-FTC (hazard ratio 0.12).
GBD AMR 2019.paper
Resistance & stewardship

GBD AMR 2019 (2022)

Global burden of bacterial antimicrobial resistance in 2019: a systematic analysis

In 2019, 1.27 million deaths were attributable to bacterial AMR and 4.95 million were associated with it.
DoxyPEP.paper
STI prevention

DoxyPEP (2023)

Postexposure Doxycycline to Prevent Bacterial Sexually Transmitted Infections

STIs per quarter: 10.7% with doxy-PEP vs 31.9% with standard care in the PrEP cohort (relative risk 0.34).
TRUNCATE-TB.paper
TB

TRUNCATE-TB (2023)

Treatment Strategy for Rifampin-Susceptible Tuberculosis

Noninferior at week 96: 5.8% with the 8-week bedaquiline-linezolid strategy vs 3.9% with standard treatment, in 85 days vs 180 days of treatment.
AReSVi-006.paper
Vaccines & RSV

AReSVi-006 (2023)

Respiratory Syncytial Virus Prefusion F Protein Vaccine in Older Adults

82.6% efficacy against RSV lower respiratory tract disease: 7 cases with vaccine vs 40 with placebo.
MATISSE.paper
Vaccines & RSV

MATISSE (2023)

Bivalent Prefusion F Vaccine in Pregnancy to Prevent RSV Illness in Infants

Severe RSV illness in infants by 90 days: 6 cases after maternal vaccination vs 33 with placebo (efficacy 81.8%).
