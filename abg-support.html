Questions

What is ABG Trainer?

ABG Trainer is an arterial blood gas interpretation trainer for medical learners.

Is ABG Trainer for patient care?

No. ABG Trainer is for educational and training use only. It is not clinical decision support, and it should not be used for patient care decisions.

Do I need an account?

No account is needed.

Does practice work offline?

Practice works offline.

Does the app collect data?

The app collects no data.
